# 📦 Inventory Management System — Mobile App

A modern **Mobile Inventory Management System** designed for businesses to efficiently manage customers, suppliers, staff, stock, sales, invoices, expenses, and payments from a single application.

The application provides a centralized solution for managing day-to-day business operations while providing useful sales analytics through numerical and graphical reports.

## 🚀 Main Modules

The application consists of three primary management modules:

### 👤 Customer Management

Businesses can manage complete customer records, including:

* Add new customers
* Customer name
* Phone number
* Edit customer information
* Delete customer records
* Quickly contact customers through **WhatsApp**
* View customer-related business information

### 🚚 Supplier Management

The supplier module provides similar functionality for managing suppliers:

* Add new suppliers
* Supplier name
* Phone number
* Edit supplier information
* Delete supplier records
* Quickly contact suppliers through **WhatsApp**
* Manage supplier-related information

### 👨‍💼 Staff Management

The staff module allows businesses to maintain employee records:

* Add staff members
* Store employee information
* Edit staff records
* Delete staff records
* Manage employee information efficiently

---

## 📦 Stock Management

The inventory module helps businesses maintain accurate stock records.

### Key Features

* Add products to inventory
* Manage available stock
* Track stock quantities
* Update product information
* Automatically reduce stock when an item is sold
* Maintain up-to-date inventory levels

The automatic stock deduction helps reduce manual calculation and minimizes inventory errors.

---

## 🧾 Invoice & Billing

The application includes an integrated billing and invoice system.

### Invoice Features

* Create invoices
* Add multiple products to an invoice
* Select customer
* Enter product quantities
* Calculate invoice totals
* Select payment method
* Generate invoice
* Automatically reduce sold quantities from stock

This creates a connected workflow between **Sales → Invoice → Stock Management**.

---

## 💰 Cash & Expense Management

The application also provides business financial management features for:

* Cash management
* Business expenses
* Sales tracking
* Payment records
* Expense monitoring

This allows business owners to keep important financial information organized within one application.

---

## 📊 Sales Dashboard & Analytics

The application provides business owners with an overview of their sales performance.

Sales can be viewed in both **numerical and graphical formats**.

### Sales Reports

* 📅 Today's Sales
* 📆 Weekly Sales
* 📈 Yearly Sales
* 💳 Payment Method Sales
* 📊 Graphical Sales Reports

### Payment Method Analysis

The application can display sales based on different payment methods, helping businesses understand how customers are paying for their purchases.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this application is to **digitize and simplify everyday business management** by bringing inventory, customers, suppliers, employees, billing, expenses, cash, and sales analytics into one mobile platform.

It helps businesses reduce manual record keeping, maintain accurate stock levels, generate invoices efficiently, and monitor their sales performance through clear reports and visual analytics.
